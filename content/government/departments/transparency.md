# Transparency & Budget — Where Your Money Goes

BetterAringay.org exists to make Aringay's public money visible. Like budget.bettergov.ph/learn explains how the _national_ budget works, this page maps where to find **Aringay's own** numbers.

| Source                                        | What you find                                           | Link                                                 |

| **Bureau of Local Government Finance (BLGF)** | Annual Regular Income, IRA/NTA share, expenditure, debt | blgf.gov.ph                                          |

## 5. Five Habits (from BetterGov Budget) Applied to Aringay

1. **Check which document you're reading** — NEP proposal vs GAA law vs LGU _appropriation ordinance_.
2. **Mind the units** — national tables in thousands; LGU tables in pesos.
3. **Appropriation ≠ spending** — cross-check with obligation & disbursement reports.
4. **Renames hide continuity** — a “new” program may be a rebranded SB project.
5. **Read the provisions** — special provisions in ordinances fence how money may be used.

Learn the full glossary: **budget.bettergov.ph/learn** (MOOE, SARO, NCA, UACS, COFOG).
