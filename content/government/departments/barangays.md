# Barangays of Aringay — 24 Communities, One Municipality

Aringay is a 2nd-class coastal municipality with **24 barangays** covering **84.54 km²** along Lingayen Gulf. Population **50,380** (2020 Census) / **50,786** (2024 POPCEN) — density **596/km²**. ZIP code **2503** for all barangays.

## How to Contact a Barangay

1. **Visit the barangay hall** of your residence — Mon–Fri 8AM–5PM.
2. **Bring** valid ID + proof of residency.
3. **Pay** ₱50–100 for barangay clearance (typically same-day).
4. For assistance, contact the **Municipal Hall**: Marcos Avenue, Poblacion, Aringay 2503 — **(072) 607-9531** — **lgu_aringay@yahoo.com**.

Next **Barangay & SK Elections: Nov 2, 2026** (4-year term per RA 12232).

---

## What You Can Do at the Barangay

- Barangay clearance, ID, and residency certification
- First-aid and referral to Rural Health Unit
- Peace & order (Barangay Tanod), dispute mediation (Lupon)
- Senior, PWD, solo parent registration and aid distribution
- Disaster preparedness and evacuation

Find your barangay hall via the **LGU Aringay** official site **aringay.gov.ph** and the **Barangay Directory** (opens per-barangay pages with officials & contacts).

## Barangay Officials

Each barangay has a **Punong Barangay**, 7 **Kagawads**, SK Chair, and Secretary/Treasurer. 100% of Aringay's 24 barangays have documented elected-officials records (Barangay Directory, 2026). For current rosters, see the **Directory** pages per barangay — they link to the LGU's official postings and COMELEC BSKE results.
