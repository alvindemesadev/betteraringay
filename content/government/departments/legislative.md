# Sangguniang Bayan of Aringay — Legislative Department

The **Sangguniang Bayan (SB)** is Aringay's municipal council — the legislative body that enacts ordinances, approves resolutions, and reviews the annual budget.

## Mandate & Powers (Local Government Code, RA 7160)

- **Enact ordinances** — local laws on taxation, land use, health, peace & order, markets, transport
- **Appropriate funds** — approve annual budget / supplemental budgets
- **Authorize contracts** and franchises
- **Confirm appointments** and conduct oversight hearings
- **Declare** position on national issues via resolutions

---

## How an Ordinance Becomes Law in Aringay

1. **Proposal** — filed by councilor, mayor, or petition.
2. **First reading** — title read, referred to committee.
3. **Committee hearings** — public hearing if required (notice via hall & Facebook).
4. **Second reading** — debate, amendments.
5. **Third reading** — final vote (majority of _all_ members).
6. **Mayor's action** — sign or veto (line-item veto not for LGU; veto can be overridden by 2/3 SB vote).
7. **Post & publish** — 10-day posting + publication for penal ordinances; take effect.

> Hearings are **public** — attend at Municipal Hall, Sangguniang Bayan Session Hall, Marcos Avenue, Poblacion.

## Sessions

- **Regular sessions**: Weekly, Sangguniang Bayan Hall — check SB Facebook / bulletin for schedule (typically Monday or Tuesday)
- **Special sessions**: called by mayor or majority

Citizens may request to be heard via **Privilege Speech / Public Consultation** — submit written request to SB Secretary 3 days prior.
