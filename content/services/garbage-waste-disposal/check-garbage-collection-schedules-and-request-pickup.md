# Garbage Collection Schedules & Request Pickup — Municipality of Aringay

The Municipality of Aringay manages **regular garbage collection** via **MENRO (Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office)** together with the 24 barangays. Trucks cover Poblacion and barangay clusters on a **zone schedule** — segregate before you put bins out.

---

## 1) Regular Collection (Household)

**How it works**

- Schedules vary **by zone/barangay cluster** (typically **2–3 times per week**; Poblacion more frequent; upland Basca/Gallano less frequent). Coastal barangays (Dulao, Samara, San Eugenio) have beach waste protocols.
- Trucks collect **biodegradable, recyclable, residual, special** (if properly bagged/labeled) — **segregation at source mandatory** per RA 9003.
- No collection on **declared holidays/typhoons** — MENRO/Barangay announces catch-up.

**Where to check**

1. **Barangay Hall** — posted schedule at hall + purok leaders
2. **Barangay FB / GC** — most barangays post delays/reschedules
3. **MENRO / Municipal Hall** bulletin (072) 607-9531, `facebook.com/betteraringay`, `aringay.gov.ph`

> Tip: Post your zone's calendar on gate/fridge — put bins out **early morning of collection day** only.

---

## 2) Special / Bulk Pickup (Non-Routine)

For **bulky waste**: furniture, appliances, construction debris, yard trimmings, large e-waste — **not** part of regular truck.

**How to request**

1. Go to **Barangay Hall** (or call) → Barangay Solid Waste / Environment Committee
2. Give **type, volume, address, contact**
3. Barangay endorses to **MENRO** for special truck scheduling (may be grouped with zone pick-ups)
4. Minimal fee may apply for large construction debris (per ordinance; MENRO confirms)

**Needs special pickup**

- Mattresses, large furniture, appliances, e-waste
- Construction debris (hollow blocks, wood — small household amounts only; contractor debris is contractor's responsibility)
- Hazardous household (paints, chemicals, batteries — handled under special collection, see separate guide)

## 4) Quick Tips

- Use **covered bins/bags**; tie properly to avoid stray animals and beach scattering (coastal zones).
- Recyclables → barangay **MRF (Materials Recovery Facility)** or accredited junkshop (Poblacion/Dulao area) — MENRO list.
- **Do not burn** waste (RA 9003 / Clean Air Act).
- During **rainy season / southwest monsoon**, expect delays — follow MENRO/barangay posts.

---

## 5) Businesses & Establishments

- Segregate and keep **proof of disposal** (OR/hauling contracts) for MENRO/BFP inspection
- Beach resorts/eateries in Dulao/Samara: coordinate **extra coastal pickups** with MENRO + Barangay

**Key Reminder:** Follow your zone's schedule and request special pickups — keeps Aringay clean, prevents Lingayen Gulf pollution, flooding, and pests.
