# Special Collection for Hazardous & E-Waste — Municipality of Aringay

Hazardous and e-waste **cannot** go with regular truck. **Barangay halls + MENRO** coordinate **special pickups** and drop-offs.

---

## 1) What Counts

| Category                | Examples                                                                      | Notes                                           |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Household Hazardous** | Batteries, fluorescent bulbs, paints/solvents, pesticides, cleaning chemicals | Toxic to soil/water, especially coastal         |
| **E-Waste**             | Phones, computers, printers, TVs, chargers, cables                            | Contains lead/mercury                           |
| **Household Medical**   | Syringes/sharps, soiled bandages                                              | Turn over to **RHU Aringay** / BHS — do not bin |

---

## 2) How to Request — Aringay

1. **Contact Barangay Hall** — Environment / Solid Waste desk → give **type, volume, address**
2. **Barangay → MENRO** — MENRO schedules **special truck** or directs to next **municipal/Barangay collection drive**; may refer to **accredited junkshop/Recycler** (Poblacion/Dulao list)
3. **Prepare items** — sealed, labeled boxes/bags; do **not** mix with household garbage or break bulbs
4. **Follow schedule** — put out only when instructed or bring to drop-off (Barangay Hall / Municipal Hall MENRO)

---

## 3) Other Options

- **Barangay e-waste drives** — often quarterly or during **Kilawen Festival** clean-ups
- **School/MSWDO drives** — occasional e-waste donation drives with MENRO
- **Accredited junkshops** — some take e-waste for recycling; ask MENRO for accredited list (avoids informal dumping)

## 5) Household Tips

- Keep a **separate box** for batteries/bulbs/e-waste (don't stockpile long)
- Don't dismantle devices or break bulbs
- **Bundle with neighbors** — barangay schedules faster for group requests in same purok

**Reminder:** Never mix hazardous/e-waste with regular garbage — coordinate with **Barangay Hall + MENRO** for safe collection and to protect Lingayen Gulf.
