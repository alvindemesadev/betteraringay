# Access Educational Support Programs — Municipality of Aringay

Below are **LGU Aringay-facilitated** programs you can use: school supplies, student employment (SPES), scholarships/assistance, and DSWD aid — all coordinated via **MSWDO, PESO Aringay, and DepEd La Union**.

---

## 1) School Supplies & SEF Support

**What it is**

- **Special Education Fund (SEF)**-funded school supplies for public learners, distributed per school year via DepEd Aringay districts

**How to access**

1. Watch your school's advisory and **DepEd La Union — Aringay Districts** for distribution schedules (early school year, June–August)
2. Learners/parents claim supplies at the school on announced date

**Links**

- DepEd Schools Division of La Union — depedlaunion.com / FB: DepEd Tayo La Union
- News: watch `facebook.com/betteraringay` and barangay elementary school FB pages

---

## 2) Study Space & Digital Access

**What it is**

- **No city e-Library** in a 2nd-class municipality — Aringay learners use **school libraries, barangay halls with Wi-Fi (where available), and Municipal Hall / MSWDO referrals** for computer/printing needs
- **DMMMSU Open University / public libraries in San Fernando City** for tertiary learners

**How to access**

1. Ask your **barangay hall / elementary school** about available study space and internet
2. For tertiary: visit **DMMMSU libraries** or **Provincial Library, San Fernando City**

> For printing/internet aid, MSWDO may refer students to DSWD assistance if qualified.

---

## 3) Student Employment (SPES) via PESO Aringay + DOLE

**What it is**

- **Special Program for the Employment of Students (SPES)** — DOLE program for poor but deserving **students/OSY 15–30** to work during breaks and earn for school
- Implemented locally via **PESO Aringay** with **DOLE La Union Field Office**

**How to access**

1. Follow **PESO Aringay** (Municipal Hall) and **DOLE Region I** FB for **SPES application windows** (usually summer/Christmas breaks)
2. Prepare: valid ID, school registration, Barangay Certificate of Indigency, birth certificate
3. Submit at **PESO Aringay, Municipal Hall, Marcos Ave** or DOLE La Union; attend orientation if shortlisted

**Links**

- PESO Aringay — Municipal Hall (072) 607-9531
- DOLE Region I / La Union Field Office — SPES 2025 guidelines

---

## 4) LGU-linked Scholarships & Incentives

**What it is**

- **Municipality of Aringay Educational Assistance** (MSWDO/Mayor's Office) — periodic cash aid for JHS/SHS/college, separate from school-based grants
- **Provincial Government of La Union** scholarships

**How to access**

1. Monitor **MSWDO Aringay** and **Office of the Mayor** for **official calls** (bulletin at Municipal Hall + FB)
2. Prepare residency + academic docs; submit by deadline; attend screening

---

## 5) Educational / Cash Assistance (MSWDO-facilitated with DSWD)

**What it is**

- **DSWD AICS Educational Assistance** — nationally funded, locally coordinated via **MSWDO Aringay** for venue/beneficiary management

**How to access**

1. Watch **DSWD Field Office I** and **MSWDO Aringay** for payout schedules (often at Municipal Hall or San Fernando)
2. Bring student ID, Certificate of Enrollment/Registration, Barangay Indigency, valid ID

---

## Quick Directory — Aringay

- **MSWDO Aringay** — educational assistance, SPES coordination — Municipal Hall, Marcos Ave, Poblacion — (072) 607-9531
- **PESO Aringay** — SPES, job fairs, youth employment — Municipal Hall
- **DepEd La Union — Aringay Districts** — school memos, SEF supplies — respective elementary schools + Division Office San Fernando
- **DMMMSU / TESDA La Union** — college/TVET scholarships

---

## Tips

- **Stack benefits** where allowed: SEF supplies **+** SPES stipend **+** school/CHED scholarship
- Keep ready: **PSA Birth Cert**, **Barangay Residency/Indigency**, **School ID**, **Report Cards/COE**, **2×2 photos**
- Turn on notifications for **PESO Aringay** and **Municipality FB** so you don't miss windows
