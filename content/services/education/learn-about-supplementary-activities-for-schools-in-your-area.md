# Supplementary Activities & Student Organizations — Municipality of Aringay

Clubs, competitions, service groups, and enrichment programs for learners in **Aringay, La Union** — via your school + **DepEd La Union – Aringay Districts**.

---

## Where to Look First (Aringay)

1. **Your school's FB / bulletin** — Aringay Central ES, Aringay National HS, San Eugenio ES/HS, Dulao ES, etc. post club fairs & tryouts
2. **DepEd Schools Division of La Union — Aringay Districts (I & II)**
   - Division site: https://www.depedlaunion.com (regional: deped.gov.ph)
   - District office + school guidance counselors announce division contests
3. **Municipal Hall / MSWDO / Sangguniang Kabataan (SK)** — youth camps, clean-ups, sports fests, Kilawen Festival student showcases

## Academic & Interest Clubs (Common in Aringay Schools)

- **STEM/Science/Math** — science fairs, investigatory projects, robotics/ICT (division fairs via DepEd La Union)
- **ArPan / Social Science** — quiz bowls, Kilawen heritage walks
- **MAPEH / Arts** — choral, dance troupe (Kilawen street dancing), theater
- **TLE/ICT** — coding basics, agri-fishery entrepreneurship (link to MAO)
- **Sports** — tryouts via MAPEH/sports coordinator; **Aringay Town Fiesta & La Union provincial meets**

## Enrichment & Alternative Pathways

- **ALS (Alternative Learning System)** — for OSY / flexible learners
  - Contact **ALS Aringay / DepEd La Union ALS Focal** via your barangay elementary school or MSWDO Municipal Hall
- **Municipal / SK Youth Programs** — SK camps, sports fests, clean-up drives (coastal Dulao/Samara, upland Basca/Gallano)
- **DRRM Youth** — YES-O + RCY + school DRRM with **MDRRMO Aringay** (earthquake/fire drills)

---

## How to Join (Quick Steps)

1. Scan your school's bulletin/FB during first month of classes for club fairs
2. Prepare: school ID + parent consent (if needed)
3. Attend orientation/tryouts; note meeting schedules
4. Commit to service hours/outputs
5. Track division announcements via **DepEd La Union** and your school

---

## Parent/Guardian Pointers

- Ask about **safety, meeting times, fees** — most are free/school-supported
- Balance academics + extracurriculars with a weekly schedule
- Encourage documentation roles (secretary, treasurer) for leadership
