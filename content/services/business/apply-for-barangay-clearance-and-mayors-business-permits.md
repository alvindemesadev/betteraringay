# Apply for Barangay Clearance and Mayor's Business Permit — Municipality of Aringay, La Union

Start a legal business in Aringay: get your **Barangay Business Clearance** from your barangay, then your **Mayor's / Business Permit** from the Municipal Hall. This guide is for sole proprietors, partnerships, and corporations opening a main office or branch in the 24 barangays of Aringay.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Where**           | Barangay Hall (for clearance) → BPLO, Municipal Hall, Marcos Avenue, Poblacion, Aringay 2503                                                           |
| **When**            | Mon–Fri 8AM–5PM; business permit renewal **Jan 1–20** annually; new permits year-round                                                                 |

## Step 1: Register Your Business Name / Entity

**Prepare documents:**

- Business name — check via [DTI BNRS](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/) (sole proprietor) or [SEC eSPARC](https://espac.sec.gov.ph/) (partnership/corporation)
- For partnership/corp: Notarized Articles of Partnership/Incorporation, IDs/TINs of partners/directors, office address, capital structure

**File online:** DTI BNRS or SEC eSPARC, pay fees.

**Output:** DTI Certificate of Business Name Registration **or** SEC Certificate of Incorporation/Partnership

---

## Step 2: Secure Barangay Business Clearance

Go to the **Barangay Hall** where your business will operate (any of the 24: Poblacion, San Eugenio, Dulao, etc.).

**Requirements:**

- Filled Barangay Business Clearance application
- DTI/SEC certificate + valid ID
- Proof of address: Lease Contract (if renting) or Tax Declaration / Title (if owned)
- Community Tax Certificate (Cedula) — available at Municipal Treasurer's Office or barangay
- Sketch of business location

**Tip:** Barangay business clearance is different from barangay _personal_ clearance. Ask for the business version.

---

## Step 3: Apply for Mayor's / Business Permit at BPLO

Go to **Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO), Municipal Hall, Marcos Avenue, Poblacion, Aringay, La Union 2503** — trunk (072) 607-9531, email lgu_aringay@yahoo.com.

**Requirements:**

- Barangay Business Clearance (from Step 2)
- DTI/SEC/CDA certificate + Cedula
- Contract of Lease **or** Certificate of Occupancy / Building Permit (if owned)
- Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSCI) from BFP Aringay — BPLO will route you
- Sanitary Permit (MHO/RHU) for food/health businesses — health card for staff if required
- Zoning clearance (MPDC) if new construction/renovation
- Previous permit & ORs (for renewal)

> BPLO runs a **Business One-Stop Shop (BOSS)** every January at the Municipal Hall quadrangle/plaza — one table for BPLO, Treasurer, MHO, MPDC, BFP, MENRO.

---

## Step 4: Register with BIR

File at the **BIR Revenue District Office (RDO 003 – San Fernando City, La Union)** where Aringay is covered.

- Fill [BIR Form 1901](https://www.bir.gov.ph/) (sole) / 1903 (partnership/corp)
- Submit DTI/SEC + Mayor's Permit + valid IDs + lease/occupancy docs
- Pay registration fee + Documentary Stamp Tax

**Within 30 days of BIR Certificate (Form 2303):**

- Apply for Authority to Print Receipts (Form 1906) or CAS/e-invoicing
- Register Books of Accounts

---

## Step 5: Register as Employer (if hiring)

Enroll with:

- [SSS](https://www.sss.gov.ph/)
- [PhilHealth](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/)
- [Pag-IBIG](https://www.pagibigfund.gov.ph/)

## Process Flow (Summary)

1. DTI/SEC name check → register business
2. Barangay Hall → business clearance (same day)
3. BPLO Municipal Hall → Mayor's Permit → routed to MTO/BFP/MHO/MPDC as needed → pay at Municipal Treasurer → claim permit
4. BIR RDO San Fernando → Form 1901/1903 → ATP/Books
5. SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG if with employees

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Barangay clearance vs Mayor's permit — do I need both?**
A: Yes. Barangay clearance first (your barangay consents), then Mayor's permit (municipality authorizes). BPLO will not accept without barangay clearance.

**Q: How much is the Mayor's permit?**
A: Based on capitalization (new) or gross sales (renewal) + local tax ordinance rates + regulatory fees. Assessment printed at BPLO before payment.

**Q: Can I apply online?**
A: Aringay is primarily walk-in at BPLO. For DTI/SEC/BIR, online via BNRS/eSPARC/BIR eAFS. Follow `facebook.com/lgu.aringay.official` and `aringay.gov.ph` for any BOSS online pre-assessment announcements.

**Q: Open in a barangay far from Poblacion (e.g., Gallano, Basca)?**
A: Still get barangay clearance at that barangay hall, but Mayor's permit is only at Municipal Hall, Poblacion.
