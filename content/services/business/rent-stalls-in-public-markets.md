# Rent Stalls in the Aringay Public Market — Municipality of Aringay

Secure a **public market stall** in **Poblacion Public Market, Aringay** for meat, fish, vegetables, dry goods, eatery, or sari-sari. Managed by the **Market Administrator / Office of the Municipal Treasurer and Office of the Mayor**, under Sangguniang Bayan market ordinance.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Where**           | Market Administrator's Office, Aringay Public Market, Poblacion (adjacent to Municipal Hall, Marcos Ave)                                                                       |
| **When**            | Mon–Fri 8AM–5PM; market open daily ~4AM–6PM                                                                                                                                    |
| **Cost**            | Monthly stall rental per ordinance (by section: fish/meat/vegetables/dry goods/eatery) + Mayor's Permit + sanitary & garbage fees. Rate sheet posted at Market Office and MTO. |
| **Processing Time** | 1–3 days after inspection + SB approval if needed (for permanent stalls)                                                                                                       |

## Types of Stalls (Poblacion Public Market)

- **Wet section:** fish (Dulao/Samara catch), meat
- **Vegetables & fruits** (upland barangays: Basca, Gallano, Macabato)
- **Dry goods & sari-sari**
- **Eatery / kainan / carinderia**
- **Temporary / ambulant** (fiesta / Kilawen Festival)

> Aringay is coastal (Lingayen Gulf) + agricultural — most vendors combine fish + upland produce.

## How to Apply (Step-by-Step)

1. **Inquiry & site check**
   Visit **Market Administrator, Aringay Public Market** — ask for vacancy map. Check section, stall size, rental rate, and house rules.

2. **Prepare requirements**
   - Valid ID + Cedula
   - Barangay Clearance (business — from your barangay hall)
   - DTI/SEC registration (if business name) + BIR 2303 (if any)
   - Mayor's/Business Permit (or apply in parallel at BPLO)
   - Sanitary Permit / health card (RHU) for food stalls
   - Fire Safety (BFP) if eatery/cooking

3. **File application**
   Submit filled **Market Stall Application Form** + requirements at Market Office → evaluation + site inspection

4. **Assessment & award**
   MTO/Market Office + MPDC/Mayor's Office assesses rental + fees → Mayor's approval / SB confirmation (per ordinance for permanent stalls)

5. **Payment & contract**
   Pay initial rental + fees at **Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO)**, sign **Lease/Contract of Occupancy** (good for 1 year, renewable)

6. **Occupancy**
   Issued **Market Stall Permit / Lease** → occupy, display permit, follow market rules (hours, waste segregation, MENRO).

## Rules & Operations

- **Hours:** follow market schedule; no overnight sleeping in stalls (unless watchman-allowed)
- **Waste:** segregate per RA 9003 — wet/dry; MENRO collection at designated points
- **Subleasing:** not allowed without written Mayor's approval per ordinance
- **Renewal:** annual, alongside Mayor's Permit renewal each January
- **Kilawen Festival / Fiesta:** temporary stalls at Municipal Plaza require separate **Festival Stall Permit** from BPLO/Tourism Office

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much is a stall?**
A: Varies by section and size — see rate posted at Market Office. Fish/meat stalls higher due to water/drainage; dry goods lower. Ask for the **latest Sangguniang Bayan Market Ordinance rate sheet**.

**Q: Can I choose my barangay's products only?**
A: You can sell any allowed goods, but priority for Aringay farmers/fisherfolk during harvest/peak catch — coordinate with **MAO (Municipal Agriculturist)** for supply linking.

**Q: What if I only need a fiesta stall for Kilawen?**
A: Apply for **Temporary Stall Permit** at BPLO/Tourism Office — shorter requirements, fees per day/week at Municipal Plaza.
