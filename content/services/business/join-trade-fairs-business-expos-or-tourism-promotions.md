# Join Trade Fairs, Business Expos & Tourism Promotions — Municipality of Aringay

Showcase Aringay products — **rice, vegetables, fish/seafood, processed foods, handicrafts, beach tourism** — at **Kilawen Festival, Aringay Town Fiesta, La Union expos, and DTI-OTOP fairs**. For MSMEs, farmers/fisherfolk, resort operators, tour guides, and community livelihood groups in the 24 barangays.

---

## Who Can Join

- **MSMEs** (sole, partnership, corp, coop) with DTI/SEC/CDA + Mayor's Permit from Aringay
- **Agri & fishery groups** (Basca, Gallano, Macabato, Manga, Pangao-aoan, coastal barangays Dulao/Samara/San Eugenio)
- **Tourism enterprises** (Aringay Beach resorts, eateries, transport, tour guides)
- **Women/youth/community associations** accredited by LGU/MSWDO

Priority: OTOP-branded, export-ready, food-safe (FDA when needed), and Kilawen/tourism-aligned products.

---

## Event Types You'll See

- **Aringay Kilawen Festival Trade Fair** — Municipal Plaza, Poblacion (usually alongside fiesta — watch LGU FB for dates)
- **La Union Unity Trade Fair / Agricultural Fairs** — Provincial Capitol, City of San Fernando
- **DTI La Union OTOP Hub & Negosyo Center fairs** — San Fernando / Bauang / Agoo
- **Barangay & Fiesta Bazaars** — Poblacion and coastal barangays during patronal fiestas

---

## How to Participate (Step-by-Step)

1. **Prepare documents**
   DTI/SEC/CDA, BIR 2303, current **Mayor's/Business Permit (Aringay)**, FDA/LTO for food/cosmetics if applicable

2. **Pre-register**
   Submit **Intent Form** at **BPLO or Municipal Tourism/Agriculture Office**, Municipal Hall, Marcos Ave, or at **Negosyo Center Aringay** (DTI La Union satellite) — include product list, prices, photos, booth needs (table, outlet watts)

3. **Screening**
   LGU/DTI checks quality, labeling, capacity. Accepted → pay **booth fee (often waived/subsidized for Aringay MSMEs)** and sign guidelines

4. **Pre-event readiness**
   Banners, price cards, QR, packaging, staffing, POS (GCash/Maya), receipts, cold chain for fish/processed foods

5. **Event days**
   Ingress early, follow fire/safety/electrical/waste rules, capture leads (name, phone, FB)

6. **Post-event (72 hours)**
   Thank-you messages, price lists, confirm orders, track KPIs

## Safety & Compliance

- Follow **BFP** fire/electrical limits, **MHO** food safety, **MENRO** waste segregation at venue
- Food: maintain **cold chain**, proper labeling (ingredients, expiry, allergens)
- Respect IP (no counterfeit, no copyrighted music/images without permission)
