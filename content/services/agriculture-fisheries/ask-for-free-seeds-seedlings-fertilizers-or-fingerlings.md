# Ask for Free Seeds, Seedlings, Fertilizers or Fingerlings — Municipality of Aringay

Request free agricultural and fishery inputs from the **Municipal Agriculturist Office (MAO)**, Municipal Hall, Marcos Ave, Poblacion — support for rice, vegetables, and Lingayen Gulf fisherfolk across the 24 barangays.

---

## Types of Inputs

- **Seeds:** palay (inbred/hybrid via DA), vegetable seeds (up to 5 kinds)
- **Seedlings:** vegetable, fruit, mangrove (via MENRO for coastal)
- **Fertilizers:** complete/urea/organic per DA allocation (limited bags per farmer, per guidelines)
- **Fingerlings:** tilapia/bangus for fishponds and communal waters — via BFAR I / Provincial Agriculturist

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Be RSBSA-Registered

Visit **MAO** with valid ID + barangay certificate + proof of farming/fishing (tax declaration, lease, or barangay certification). MAO will enroll you in **RSBSA (Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture)** if not yet listed.

### Step 2: Request

Fill **Request Slip for Agricultural Inputs** at MAO — indicate barangay (e.g., Basca, Gallano, Dulao), area (hectares), crop/pond size.

### Step 3: Schedule & Claim

MAO posts distribution schedule at Municipal Hall, barangay halls, and `facebook.com/betteraringay`. Claim on schedule with ID. Fingerlings collected at MAO/BFAR-demo pond or barangay pond.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Not yet RSBSA?**
A: MAO will register you — bring ID + barangay certificate indicating you farm/fish.

**Q: Upland barangays like Basca/Gallano vs coastal Dulao/Samara — same?**
A: MAO clusters distribution; upland more seeds/fertilizer, coastal more fingerlings/fishing gear via BFAR.

**Q: How many bags/kits?**
A: Per DA/BFAR guidelines — typically 1 kit per farmer per season, limited by stock. Posted per distribution.
