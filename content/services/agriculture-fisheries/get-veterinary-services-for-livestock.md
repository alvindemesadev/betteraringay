# Veterinary Services for Livestock — Municipality of Aringay

Free/basic veterinary services for livestock and poultry via **Municipal Agriculturist Office (MAO) + Provincial Veterinary Office, La Union** — deworming, vaccination, and advice for backyard raisers in the 24 barangays.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information | Details                                                                                                           |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**   | MAO, Municipal Hall, Marcos Ave, Poblacion; field visits to barangays (Basca, Gallano, Macabato, upland clusters) |
| **When**    | Mon–Fri 8AM–5PM; field missions often Tue–Thu per barangay schedule                                               |
| **Cost**    | Basic consultation free; medicines/vaccines free when DOH/DA stocks available (limited)                           |
| **Who**     | Backyard raisers of carabao/cattle, goat, swine, poultry in Aringay                                               |

---

## Services Offered

- **Animal health check** — clinical exam, diagnosis
- **Vaccination** — hog cholera, hemorrhagic septicemia (cattle/carabao), Newcastle (poultry) — per Provincial Vet schedule
- **Deworming & vitamins** (livestock/poultry)
- **Artificial Insemination (AI)** referral via Provincial Vet — for cattle/carabao
- **Referral** for laboratory (Provincial Veterinary Laboratory, San Fernando) if needed

---

## How to Avail

### Step 1: Report

Visit **MAO** or **Barangay Hall** — tell barangay Kagawad on Agriculture / BHW. Give: animal type, heads, symptoms, barangay + purok, contact.

### Step 2: Field Visit or Clinic

MAO schedules **barangay visit** or asks you to bring animal to **MAO clinic** (small animals) — large animals visited onsite. For AI: schedule via Provincial Vet.

### Step 3: Treatment & Advice

Follow MAO vet's prescription, quarantine advice, and re-visit schedule. Keep **Livestock Card** (MAO issues) for record.

## Requirements

- [ ] Valid ID + Barangay Certificate (proof you reside/raise in Aringay)
- [ ] Animal description (breed, age, heads)
- [ ] For AI: proof of heat cycle (vet will check)
