# Farming & Fishing Training — Municipality of Aringay

Join free training via **MAO + TESDA + DA/BFAR Region I + DTI Negosyo Center La Union** — palay, vegetables, coastal fisheries, and farm entrepreneurship for Aringay farmers/fisherfolk (24 barangays).

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information | Details                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**   | MAO training hall, Municipal Hall, Marcos Ave; demo farms in Basca/Gallano; coastal demo in Dulao/Samara; TESDA La Union (San Fernando) |
| **When**    | Per MAO calendar — posted at Municipal Hall, barangay halls, `facebook.com/betteraringay` (often pre-planting season & off-season)      |
| **Cost**    | Free (LGU/DA/BFAR/TESDA-funded); meals/materials may be provided per program                                                            |
| **Who**     | RSBSA farmers/fisherfolk, youth, women's groups, cooperatives                                                                           |

---

## Training Topics (Aringay context)

- **Palay:** inbred/hybrid, palayCheck, soil health, pest management (DA)
- **Vegetables:** high-value crops for upland (Basca, Gallano) — trellis, drip, GAP
- **Fisheries:** sustainable gillnet, tilapia/bangus grow-out, fish processing (BFAR I) — Lingayen Gulf
- **Livestock/Poultry:** backyard management, AI awareness, biosecurity (ASF prevention)
- **Entrepreneurship:** costing, packaging, market linkage via DTI Negosyo Center + MAO

---

## How to Join

1. **Register interest** at **MAO** or **Barangay Hall** — fill **Training Interest Slip** (name, barangay, sector: farmer/fisher/youth, contact)
2. **RSBSA** check — MAO enrolls if not yet
3. **Wait for schedule** — MAO texts/calls + posts at barangay hall
4. **Attend** — bring ID, notebook, boots/hat for field demo; certificate issued after
