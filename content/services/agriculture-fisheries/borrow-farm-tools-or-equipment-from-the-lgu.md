# Borrow Farm Tools & Equipment — Municipality of Aringay

Borrow **LGU-owned farm tools and equipment** via **MAO** — for small farmers/coops in the 24 barangays. Inventory limited; scheduling required.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information | Details                                                                                                       |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**   | MAO, Municipal Hall, Marcos Ave, Poblacion — equipment yard                                                   |
| **When**    | Mon–Fri 8AM–5PM; booking ahead (first-come, per barangay cluster)                                             |
| **Cost**    | Free rental for Aringay farmers (return in good condition); diesel/fuel may be borrower’s share per ordinance |
| **Who**     | RSBSA farmers, coops, irrigators' associations                                                                |

## Available (Typical Inventory — ask MAO for current list)

- **Hand tractor / kuliglig** (1–2 units)
- **Thresher / reaper** (seasonal)
- **Water pump / irrigation pump**
- **Hand tools** (sprayers, etc. — limited)
- **Fisheries gear** (for demo, not long-term — via BFAR)

_Lists posted at MAO; equipment varies by grant cycle (DA/Provincial)._

---

## How to Borrow

### Step 1: Request

Visit **MAO** → fill **Equipment Borrow Slip**: name, barangay, RSBSA, tool needed, dates, area/hectares, purpose.

### Step 2: Schedule & Clearance

MAO checks **availability + barangay schedule** (to group nearby farms: Basca/Gallano cluster, Poblacion cluster). For large equipment, **Barangay Captain endorsement** may be required.

### Step 3: Agreement

Sign **Borrower's Agreement** — responsible for fuel, proper use, cleaning, and reporting damage. For hand tractor, licensed operator may be provided (or borrower-provided operator must be briefed).

### Step 4: Use & Return

Return on agreed date, cleaned, with **fuel refilled** (if required). MAO inspects and logs return.

## Rules

- **No sub-lending** without MAO approval
- Borrower liable for damage due to misuse/neglect
- Late return may affect future borrowing priority
- Weather/priority: emergency/planting season farmers may be prioritized
