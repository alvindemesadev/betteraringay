# Hospital for Treatment or Confinement — Municipality of Aringay Referral Guide

Aringay has **RHU (Rural Health Unit) for primary care** — for **confinement / higher-level care**, patients are **referred** to hospitals in **Agoo, San Fernando City, and Bauang** (La Union). This guide covers where to go, what to bring, and how admission works.

**Emergency: Dial (072) 607-9531 (MDRRMO/Rescue) or go directly to ER of the nearest hospital. For RHU hours: Mon–Fri 8AM–5PM.**

---

## A) Primary Care in Aringay (Start Here)

**RHU Aringay — Municipal Health Office (MHO)**

- Municipal Hall Compound, Marcos Ave, Poblacion, Aringay 2503 — via (072) 607-9531
- **Services:** triage, first aid, stabilization, referrals, free check-ups & medicines (see separate guide)
- **Emergency (within RHU hours):** go to RHU for triage → ambulance referral if needed via **MDRRMO Aringay**
- **After hours:** go straight to hospital ER (see B below) — RHU referral slip can be obtained next working day if needed for PhilHealth

## C) What to Bring (All Hospitals)

- **Valid ID** (photo & signature) + **Barangay Certificate** if proof of residency needed
- **PhilHealth** ID / MDR (Member Data Record); if dependent, proof of dependency
- **Doctor's referral / RHU admitting order** (if elective; not required for emergency)
- **HMO/Company LOA** if applicable
- **Personal necessities** for confinement (clothes, toiletries)

---

## D) Costs & PhilHealth

- **Government hospitals (LUMC, ITRMC):** fees posted at Billing; **PhilHealth** benefits applied at admission/discharge. **No Balance Billing (NBB)** for indigent/sponsored, senior citizens, PWD in government hospitals (beyond PhilHealth package, subject to eligibility & facility capacity).
- **Private hospitals:** may require **initial deposit** unless covered by HMO/PhilHealth guarantee — confirm at Admissions/Billing.

**Aringay assist:** **MSWDO** can assess for **MAIFIP/AICS** medical assistance for hospital bills — bring barangay indigency, hospital statement of account, valid ID, clinical abstract (from Municipal Hall, Marcos Ave).

---

## E) How to Avail

### Emergency

1. **Go straight to ER** (LUMC Agoo or ITRMC San Fernando) — 24/7
2. **Triage & stabilization** → tests/meds
3. If **admitted:** sign consent → ward transfer → submit IDs/PhilHealth/HMO at Admissions/Billing
4. Inform **RHU Aringay / MSWDO** next working day if you need referral slip/barangay indigency for PhilHealth/financial aid

### Elective / Planned Confinement

1. Get **doctor's admitting order** at RHU or private clinic
2. Prepare IDs, PhilHealth, HMO LOA
3. Proceed to **Admissions** of chosen hospital for room assignment & billing arrangements
