## MDRRMO Aringay Early Warning Flow

1. **MDRRMO Operations Center** (Municipal Hall) monitors **PAGASA rainfall / river gauges** + **Provincial DRRMO La Union**
2. **Alert sent** to **Barangay Captains/BDRRM → Tanods** via radio/GC → **bandillo + house-to-house** for immediate puroks
3. **Public post** on **MDRRMO FB / `facebook.com/lgu.aringay.official`** + Municipal Hall bulletin
4. **Evacuation order** if **Red Rainfall / River critical / Storm Surge Warning** — follow `learn-about-emergency-response-procedures` guide

---

## How to Access

- **Before season (May):** Visit **Barangay Hall** to confirm **family contact + evacuation center assignment** is listed in **Barangay Master List**
- **During weather:** Check **PAGASA + MDRRMO FB every 3 hours**; keep **power bank charged**, **AM/FM radio** (MDRRMO recommends)
- **SMS/Call:** Ensure **Barangay Secretary has your mobile** — Tanods call vulnerable households (PWD/seniors) first
- **Go Bag:** IDs, meds, flashlight, water, cash — at door during Signal 1+
