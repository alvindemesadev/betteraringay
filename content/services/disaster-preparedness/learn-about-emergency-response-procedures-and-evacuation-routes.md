# Emergency Response & Evacuation Routes — Municipality of Aringay

Know **MDRRMO Aringay response procedures** and **evacuation routes/centers** for flood, storm surge/tsunami, earthquake, and landslide — 24 barangays.

## Emergency Response Procedures (MDRRMO)

1. **Monitoring:** MDRRMO monitors **PAGASA rainfall / river level** + **PHIVOLCS** + **Provincial DRRMO La Union**
2. **Alert:** **Barangay Captains + BDRRM + Tanods** notified → **bandillo / siren / FB** + `facebook.com/lgu.aringay.official`
3. **Pre-emptive evacuation:** For **Signal 2+** or **Aringay River danger level**, MDRRMO orders **pre-emptive evacuation** — do not wait for flood
4. **Rescue:** MDRRMO/Rescue + PNP/BFP + Barangay Tanods deploy; call **(072) 607-9531** if family member needs rescue — give **exact purok/landmark + number stranded + special needs (PWD/senior/pregnant)**
5. **Centers:** MDRRMO opens **Evacuation Center** — register at **MSWDO desk** (master list of families) → food/water via MSWDO/DSWD
6. **All-clear:** MDRRMO announces return only after **MDRRMO + Barangay** declares safe — check for **downed lines / debris**

## Evacuation Routes (General — follow posted map at your barangay hall)

- **Coastal (Dulao/Samara/San Eugenio):** **East / upland** via **Barangay roads to national highway then to higher ground** (Basca/Gallano foothills or designated center) — **away from sea**, not parallel to coastline
- **Poblacion / River low-lying:** **West/East to higher purok** or to **Municipal Evacuation Center** (Poblacion Covered Court / Central ES)
- **Upland (Basca/Gallano):** **Avoid slopes** during heavy rain → to **ridge/central barangay hall** (landslide safe zone)

> **Exact route + center assigned per puroks** — posted at **Barangay Hall** + MDRRMO. If unsure, ask **Barangay Captain/BDRRM** before rainy season.
