## Types of Drills & Planning

- **Earthquake / Tsunami (coastal Dulao/Samara/San Eugenio):** duck-cover-hold → evacuation to **higher ground** (upland Basca/Gallano routes or designated hill) — **not** beach
- **Flood (Aringay River / low-lying Poblacion):** early warning → move to **Evacuation Centers** (central elementary schools, barangay halls, covered courts)
- **Fire:** BFP Aringay + MDRRMO at schools/barangay halls
- **Landslide (upland Basca/Gallano/Macabato):** slope monitoring → evacuation to safe zone

---

## How to Join

1. **Register** at **Barangay Hall** (BDRRM) or **MDRRMO, Municipal Hall** — fill **Volunteer/Participant Slip** (name, barangay, purok, contact, vulnerability if PWD/senior/pregnant)
2. **Attend briefing** — MDRRMO/BFP/PNP explain **evacuation map + family reunification point + Go Bag checklist**
3. **Drill day** — follow **whistle/siren + barangay tanod** guidance; practice **evacuation route** to assigned **Evacuation Center** (posted at hall)
4. **After Action Review** — give feedback; update your **Family Evacuation Plan** (where to meet if separated)
