# Senior Citizen, Solo Parent & PWD Assistance — Municipality of Aringay

Apply for **OSCA (Senior), PDAO (PWD), and Solo Parent** IDs and assistance via **MSWDO**, Municipal Hall, Marcos Ave, Poblacion — for residents of the 24 barangays.

## Services & Benefits

- **Senior Citizen:** OSCA ID + booklet → 20% discount + VAT exemption, **Social Pension (DSWD)** for indigent seniors (P500–1,000/mo per current program), birthday cash gift if ordinanced, PhilHealth
- **PWD:** PDAO ID + booklet → discounts, PhilHealth, Assistive devices referral, **Social Pension for indigent PWD** if qualified (per LGU/DSWD)
- **Solo Parent:** Solo Parent ID (RA 11861) → discounts, 7 days parental leave (if employed), educational/medical/livelihood priority

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Register at Barangay

Visit **Barangay Hall** → Barangay Secretary / BHW → get **Barangay Certificate of Residency + Indigency** (if applicable) + **Referral Slip to MSWDO**.

### Step 2: MSWDO Assessment

Go to **MSWDO, Municipal Hall** with:

- Valid ID + PSA birth cert + Barangay Certificate + 1×1/2×2 photos
- **Senior:** proof of age (birth cert/ID)
- **PWD:** medical certificate stating disability (from RHU/MHO or private MD) + clinical abstract
- **Solo Parent:** proof (live birth cert of child, affidavit of solo parent, barangay certification, court decree if applicable)

MSWDO interviews, validates via **Social Case Study**, and issues/endorses **ID**. For DSWD pensions (senior/PWD), MSWDO endorses to **DSWD Field Office I**.

### Step 3: Release

ID claimed at **MSWDO/OSCA/PDAO**. Benefits (pension/cash) via **Municipal Hall payout** or **DSWD** schedule — watch `facebook.com/lgu.aringay.official` and barangay posts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I have both Senior and PWD?**
A: Senior ID is primary at 60+; PWD benefits profile may still be recorded at PDAO — MSWDO will advise which ID to present for transaction.

**Q: Solo Parent ID validity?**
A: 1 year, renewable — bring updated barangay cert + affidavit.

**Q: Upland/coastal barangays with transport issues?**
A: MSWDO runs **barangay outreach** (see schedule at hall) — PWD/seniors may authorize representative with SPA + IDs.
