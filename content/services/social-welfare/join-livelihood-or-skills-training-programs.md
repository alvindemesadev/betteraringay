# Livelihood & Skills Training — Municipality of Aringay

Join free **livelihood & skills training** via **MSWDO, PESO Aringay, MAO, and TESDA / DTI Negosyo Center La Union** — for seniors, women, solo parents, PWDs, youth, fisherfolk/farmers across the 24 barangays.

## Programs (Aringay context)

- **DSWD Sustainable Livelihood Program (SLP)** via **MSWDO** — micro-enterprise (sari-sari, food processing — _kilawen_, dried fish), employment facilitation
- **TESDA Skills** via **PESO Aringay** — NC II courses: cookery, bread & pastry, tailoring, welding, driving, electrical, food processing — at TESDA Provincial Training Center, San Fernando
- **DTI Pangkabuhayan / OTOP / Negosyo Center** — product development, packaging, market linking (Aringay Beach pasalubong, dried fish, handicrafts)
- **MAO livelihood** — agri/fishery (vegetable gardening, tilapia grow-out for coastal barangays)
- **DOLE Kabuhayan (DKAP) / TUPAD** via **PESO/DOLE La Union** — emergency employment + livelihood starter kits

---

## How to Join

1. **Enroll interest** at **MSWDO or PESO Aringay, Municipal Hall** — fill **Livelihood Interest Slip** (name, barangay, sector, contact, skill of interest)
2. **Assessment** — MSWDO social worker / PESO interviews; indigent/families profiled via **Listahanan** if available
3. **Schedule** — MSWDO/PESO texts/calls; TESDA/DTI schedules posted; attend at Municipal Hall or TESDA center
4. **Graduation / Starter Kit** — receive **certificate** + **kit/allowance** per program guidelines (e.g., TUPAD payout via MTO)
