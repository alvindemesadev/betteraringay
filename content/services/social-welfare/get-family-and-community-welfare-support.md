# Family & Community Welfare Support — Municipality of Aringay

Support for families and communities via **MSWDO**, in coordination with **DSWD FO I, PNP-WCPD Aringay, RHU, and Sangguniang Bayan** — counseling, referrals, and community programs for the 24 barangays.

## Services

- **Case Management & Counseling** — family conflict, VAWC, child welfare, elderly
- **Referrals:** to **RHU** (health), **PNP-WCPD** (protection), **DSWD FO I San Fernando** (AICS, residential care), **PAO** (legal referral via Provincial)
- **Community Programs:** 4Ps monitoring support (DSWD), **Parenting Sessions**, **Solo Parent & PWD support groups (PDAO/OSCA)**
- **Youth Welfare:** Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) + MSWDO youth programs, referral to **LYDO (Local Youth Development Office)**
- **Senior & PWD Community Support:** home visits, social pension endorsement (see separate guide)

---

## How to Access

### Step 1: Intake

Visit **MSWDO** or **Barangay Hall** (Barangay Secretary/BHW will refer). For protection cases: also report to **PNP Aringay WCPD** (Municipal Police Station, near Municipal Hall).

Bring:

- Valid ID + Barangay Certificate
- For child/women cases: birth cert, barangay blotter if any, clinical/Med cert if available

### Step 2: Assessment

MSWDO social worker conducts **Social Case Study / Intake Interview** — may do home visit. For urgent cases (VAWC, child abuse), MSWDO + PNP prioritize.

### Step 3: Intervention / Referral

MSWDO provides **counseling, emergency assistance, or referral** to DSWD/RAO/RHU/PNP/hospital. Follow-up visits scheduled.

---

## Special Notes

- **VAWC & Child Protection:** confidential intake at MSWDO / PNP-WCPD; **Do not** confront offender alone — seek immediate help via **barangay VAWC desk** or **PNP (117 / La Union hotline)**.
- **Mental Health:** MSWDO + RHU can refer to **DOH / La Union Provincial Health Office** mental health services.
