# Relief Goods & Cash Aid During Disasters — Municipality of Aringay

Access **family food packs / hygiene kits and AICS/cash aid** via **MSWDO + MDRRMO**, coordinated with **DSWD Field Office I** and **Provincial Government of La Union** during typhoons, floods (Lingayen Gulf/Aringay River), and other emergencies.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information | Details                                                                                                                              |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Where**   | MSWDO, Municipal Hall, Marcos Ave, Poblacion + **Barangay Halls / Evacuation Centers** (Poblacion, Dulao, San Eugenio, Samara, etc.) |
| **When**    | **During/after** disaster; MDRRMO/MSWDO posts distribution schedules at halls + FB                                                   |
| **Cost**    | Free — based on **assessment** of affected families                                                                                  |
| **Who**     | Affected families in Aringay — priority: flooded barangays, coastal (storm surge), upland (landslide)                                |

---

## Types of Aid

- **Family Food Packs:** rice, canned goods, noodles, coffee (DSWD/LGU)
- **Hygiene Kits:** soap, alcohol, diapers (if available)
- **Cash Aid (AICS):** via **MSWDO/DSWD** after social case assessment — requires indigency & disaster ID
- **Emergency Cash Transfer (ECT):** if declared state of calamity — per DSWD guidelines

---

## How to Access

### Step 1: Be Counted

Report to **Barangay Captain / BHW / Barangay Tanod** during/after disaster — **Barangay Disaster List (Master List of Affected Families)** is basis. Evacuees at **Evacuation Centers** (elementary schools / barangay halls / covered courts) are automatically listed.

### Step 2: MSWDO/MSWDO Validation

**MDRRMO** damage report + **MSWDO** house-to-house validation. Bring:

- Valid ID + Barangay Certificate of Residency/indigency
- Proof of damage (photo if any, barangay blotter if needed)

### Step 3: Distribution

- **Food packs/hygiene kits:** at **Barangay Hall / Evacuation Center** on schedule — bring ID, sign master list
- **Cash aid (AICS):** at **Municipal Hall (MSWDO) / DSWD FO I San Fernando** on scheduled payout — bring ID + barangay certificate + DSWD intake form

> Do not pay anyone — distribution is **free**. Report solicitation to **MSWDO (072) 607-9531** or **DILG**.

## Requirements (Typical)

- [ ] Valid ID of head of family / representative + SPA if not head
- [ ] Barangay Certificate of Residency / Indigency
- [ ] For cash aid: **Certificate of Disaster Victim** (from Barangay Captain, countersigned by MDRRMO/MSWDO)

## Tips

- Evacuate early when **MDRRMO** or barangay raises alert (especially coastal Dulao/Samara/San Eugenio for storm surge, low-lying Poblacion for river flooding)
- Keep **Go Bag** (IDs, birth certs, medicines, flashlight, cash)
- Follow only **official** FB: Municipality of Aringay / MDRRMO / PAGASA Region I
