# Socialized Housing & Relocation — Municipality of Aringay

Apply for **LGU/provincial/NHA socialized housing or relocation** if you are informal settler, river/coastal danger zone (Aringay River / Lingayen Gulf), or displaced by disaster/government project — via **MSWDO + MPDC + Office of the Mayor**, Municipal Hall, Marcos Ave.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information | Details                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**   | **MSWDO / MPDC / Office of the Mayor, Municipal Hall, Marcos Ave, Poblacion**                                                                                              |
| **When**    | Applications **accepted year-round**; screening per project intake (DHSUD/NHA/Provincial)                                                                                  |

| **Who**     | **Informal settler families (ISFs)**, families in **danger zones** (river easement, coastal surge zone), **displaced by disaster/project**, low-income — Aringay residents |

## Projects That May Be Available (Ask MSWDO/MPDC for Current List)

- **LGU socialized housing** (if any lot/site identified via MPDC / Sangguniang Bayan ordinance)
- **DHSUD / NHA resettlement** (regional projects — often in La Union or nearby, not always within Aringay itself)
- **Provincial Government of La Union** housing assistance (via Provincial Capitol, San Fernando)

> Aringay is 2nd-class municipality — **no large within-Aringay NHA site currently advertised**; MSWDO maintains **master list** and refers to **provincial/NHA pipelines** when intake opens.

---

## How to Apply

1. **Intake** at **MSWDO** or **Barangay Hall** (Barangay Secretary forwards) — fill **Housing Assistance Intake Form**: name, barangay, household size, tenure (owner/renter/ISF), danger zone if any, income, contact
2. **MSWDO case study + MPDC verification** — social worker visits, checks if in **danger zone** (MDRRMO map) or **ISF census**
3. **Master list** — qualified families entered in **LGU Housing Master List** (for future projects) → endorsement to **DHSUD/NHA/Provincial** when intake opens
4. **Screening** — lead agency calls shortlisted for **interview, docs, site visit**
5. **Award** — if selected: **orientation + award/occupancy** per agency terms

---

## Requirements (Typical)

- [ ] Valid IDs (head + spouse) + Barangay Certificate of Residency/Indigency
- [ ] Proof you are **ISF / danger zone / displaced** (barangay certification, MDRRMO hazard map tag, or demolition notice if any)
- [ ] Family composition (PSA birth certs of children, marriage cert if applicable)
- [ ] Income proof (payslip / barangay certificate of income / 4Ps ID if beneficiary)
- [ ] Photos of current dwelling (if requested)
