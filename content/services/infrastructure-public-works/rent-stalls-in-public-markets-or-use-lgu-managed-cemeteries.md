## How to Arrange Burial (LGU-managed)

1. **Register death** at **Municipal Civil Registrar, Municipal Hall** — bring **Medical Certificate of Death** from RHU/hospital + Valid ID of informant
2. **Arrange plot** — **General Services / MHO** assigns plot/niche at **Municipal Cemetery** (or barangay cemetery via Barangay Captain endorsement)
3. **Pay fees** at **Municipal Treasurer's Office** — burial fee, sanitary, etc. — get OR
4. **Schedule burial** — MHO/Civil Registrar issues **Burial Permit**; coordinate with **Barangay Hall** for interment schedule (especially upland Basca/Gallano with access considerations)

---

## Cemetery Rules (Aringay)

- Keep **Burial Permit + OR** for record
- Exhumation/transfer requires **Transfer Permit + Sanitary clearance** (MHO) + 5-year rule unless court/urgent
- Follow cemetery hours and MENRO waste segregation
