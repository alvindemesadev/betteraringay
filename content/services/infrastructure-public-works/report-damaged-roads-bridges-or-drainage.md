# Report Damaged Roads, Bridges or Drainage — Municipality of Aringay

Report potholes, damaged barangay roads, broken drainage/culverts, or eroded river/causeway to **Municipal Engineering Office (MEO) + MDRRMO**, Municipal Hall, Marcos Ave, Poblacion — covers all 24 barangays plus **Aringay River** spillways.

## How to Report

### Step 1: Document

Take **clear photos/video with landmark + purok/street/bridge name**, note **barangay, date/time, extent** (e.g., `1m pothole at Barangay Gallano road near spillway`, `clogged drainage in Dulao affecting 5 houses`).

### Step 2: File Report

- **Barangay Hall** — fill **Infrastructure Concern Slip** (barangay forwards to MEO) **or**
- **Municipal Hall** — **MEO front desk** — submit slip + photos + valid ID + contact
- For **Manila North Road / national road**: MEO will **endorse to DPWH La Union 1st District Engineering Office** (San Fernando)

### Step 3: Inspection & Action

**MEO + Barangay** inspects: measures, checks if barangay/municipal/provincial/national responsibility, estimates cost, programs for **Annual Investment Plan** or **Quick Response (calamity) Fund** if emergency.

- **Emergency (passable):** MDRRMO barricades/signages within hours
- **Repairs:** scheduled per procurement (bids via PhilGEPS); you get **reference number** to follow up

## Tips

- Coastal barangays (Samara, Dulao, San Eugenio): report **beach erosion / causeway washout** immediately — affects evacuation routes
- Upland (Basca, Gallano, Macabato): report **landslide-prone road cuts** before rainy season
- Do **not** attempt DIY repairs on national roads — MEO/DPWH must authorize
