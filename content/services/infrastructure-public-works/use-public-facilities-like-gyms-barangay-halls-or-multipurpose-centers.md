# Use Public Facilities: Gyms, Barangay Halls & Multipurpose Centers — Municipality of Aringay

Reserve **barangay halls, covered courts, municipal gym/covered court, and multipurpose centers** for meetings, sports, fiestas, trainings, and Kilawen Festival events.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information          | Details                                                                                                                                                                |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where to Reserve** | **Barangay Hall** (for barangay facilities) **or** **Office of the Mayor / General Services Office, Municipal Hall, Marcos Ave, Poblacion** (for municipal facilities) |
| **When**             | Mon–Fri 8AM–5PM for booking; facility open per schedule (covered courts often 6AM–9PM, barangay halls Mon–Fri 8AM–5PM)                                                 |
| **Cost**             | Many barangay halls/courts **free** for community use; **minimal fee / utility share** for private events (per ordinance); security deposit may be required            |
| **Who**              | Residents, organizations, schools, barangays, NGOs — priority: barangay constituents and LGU events                                                                    |

---

## Facilities in Aringay

- **Municipal Gym / Covered Court, Poblacion** — large events, sports fests, Kilawen Festival, BOSS one-stop shop in January
- **24 Barangay Halls + Barangay Covered Courts / Multipurpose Centers** — each barangay has hall + often a covered court (e.g., San Eugenio, Dulao, Gallano, Basca)
- **Public Elementary / High School covered courts** — via DepEd permit (separate from LGU, but LGU coordinates for community events)

---

## How to Reserve

1. **Inquiry** — Visit **Barangay Hall** (for barangay facility) or **General Services / Mayor's Office, Municipal Hall** (for municipal). Check **availability calendar**.
2. **Application** — Fill **Facility Use Application Form**: event title, organizer, date/time, expected attendees, facility needed
3. **Clearance** — For large/private events: **Barangay Captain endorsement** (if barangay) + **Mayor's Office approval**; present valid ID
4. **Payment (if any)** — at **Municipal Treasurer's Office / Barangay Treasurer** — get **OR**
5. **Use & Proper Turn-over** — follow **house rules** (noise hours, waste segregation via MENRO, no smoking, return chairs/tables cleaned)

## Rules

- **Leave no trash** — segregate per MENRO (RA 9003)
- **Noise:** observe municipal curfew/ordinance hours
- **Damage:** organizer liable; deposit may be forfeited
