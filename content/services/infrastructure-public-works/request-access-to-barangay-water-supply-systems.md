# Barangay Water Supply & Connections — Municipality of Aringay

Request new water connection, report interruption, or ask about **Barangay Water System / Aringay Water District** coverage for your purok/barangay.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information  | Details                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**    | **Municipal Engineering Office (MEO) / Barangay Water System Office** + **Aringay Water District / Barangay Water Association** (varies by barangay) — also at Barangay Hall |
| **When**     | Mon–Fri 8AM–5PM; water service 24/7 where connected                                                                                                                          |
| **Cost**     | Application/connection fee per Water District/Association rate (varies by barangay + distance) + monthly consumption                                                         |
| **Coverage** | Not all puroks have piped water — some upland puroks (Basca, Gallano) rely on communal wells/level II; Poblacion/Dulao/San Eugenio have higher coverage                      |

> Aringay is coastal + agricultural — supply may be **level III (household piped)** in Poblacion and level II (communal faucets) in some barangays; confirm at Barangay Hall.

---

## How to Request New Connection

1. **Check coverage** at **Barangay Hall** → ask if your purok is served by **Water District / Barangay Water Association**, and distance to nearest line
2. **Apply** at **Aringay Water District / Association window** (often at Barangay Hall or Municipal Hall annex) — fill **Water Connection Application**, attach:
   - Valid ID + Barangay Certificate of Residency
   - Land ownership/tax declaration or **Lessor's consent** + lease
   - Sketch of house location relative to line
3. **Site inspection** — lineman visits to confirm tapping point and estimate materials/labor
4. **Pay** connection fee at **Water District / Treasurer window** → get **OR + scheduled tapping date**
5. **Installation** — Water District team installs meter/tapping; sign **Water Service Contract**

## Report Interruption / Low Pressure

- **Barangay Water Association** first (purok taps)
- **MEO / Water District** — (072) 607-9531 trunk — for mainline
- Provide: barangay, purok, landmark, number of affected houses, since when
